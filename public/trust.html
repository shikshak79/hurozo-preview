Data and residency

where it lives, who can touch it

EU-resident by default

Runs in EU regions (eu-central-1). Your data stays in your region, or on your own infrastructure on the self-hosted plan.

Not used to train

Your conversations, canon, and data are never used to train shared models. BYO model runs against your own provider contract.

Encrypted, end to end

Encrypted in transit and at rest. Tenant isolation, secrets in a managed vault, and PII redaction on the way to any model.

Access and identity

least privilege, on by default

SSO and SCIM

SAML and OIDC single sign-on, SCIM provisioning, so access follows your directory and leavers lose it automatically.

Roles and gates

Role-based access for people, and per-worker permissions for agents. Every capability is allow, hold for a human, or off.

Full audit trail

Every action a person or a worker took, captured and exportable. Your Article 12 log, ready for an auditor.
