The model is yours

Two ways to run the model. Both without a markup on answers.

Bring your own model

Point Hurozo at your Anthropic or Google contract. You pay the model at cost, directly. Typical support conversation runs about €0.01 to €0.05 depending on the job.

Or use our tokens

No contract yet? Use Hurozo tokens at a 15% markup. The router picks the cheapest model that clears the quality bar for each task, so the bill usually still lands below a naive single-model setup.

How we price

Priced like a team, not a taxi meter.

~€0.99 per resolution

You pay for every conversation the AI closes, into a black box you do not own. The better it works, the more it costs, and 3,000 resolutions is roughly €2,970 a month, with nothing to audit.

Hurozo

You pay for the crew and the volume band. The same 3,000 conversations, flat and predictable. Route more of the desk through Hurozo and your cost per ticket falls, it doesn't rise.

Start on one worker, grow by adding volume, expand into departments. The price follows the value, not the meter.

Questions

The ones a support lead actually asks.

What counts as a conversation?

One customer thread the crew works, start to finish, no matter how many messages or which workers touch it. You pay by the band you land in, not per action.

What if I go over my band?

You move up a band, or add volume at the per-thousand rate shown on each plan. No per-ticket surprise, and you see it coming on the Bridge.

Do I pay a markup on the AI model?

Not if you bring your own, you pay the provider at cost. If you use our tokens, there is a 15% markup, but the router optimizes for the cheapest capable model, so it typically still beats running one premium model yourself.

Is my data EU-resident?

Yes, EU-resident by default, or self-hosted on the Workforce plan. Your model, your data, your rules.

What happens if I leave?

You take everything, the workers, the canon, and the full audit trail. It's a system of record you own, not a configuration you rent.
