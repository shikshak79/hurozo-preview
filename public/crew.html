The crew library

Every card is a job, done end to end.

One worker or a handful, one finished result, one gate you hold. Read a card and you know the pain it kills, how it runs, and what it costs. Start with the support crew. The same eleven players staff the other twenty-eight.

Eleven players, thirty-two crews, one frame. They all work the same way, and every one leaves a receipt. That sameness is the product.
